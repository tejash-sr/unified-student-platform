# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, personal attacks, or bullying
- Discrimination based on any protected characteristic
- Unwelcome sexual advances or comments
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces online and offline.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated fairly.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant.
